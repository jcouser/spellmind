    Copy your API key, then click Paste key. Spellmind reads it from your clipboard, tests it with your AI, keeps it in Windows' credential store and clears your clipboard. It never shows it again.

    An API key is billed by use at its company, apart from any subscription you have. No key? The ChatGPT plan sign-in needs none.

    Skip this unless your service gave you an address (OpenRouter, Groq, or a model on your own computer with Ollama or LM Studio).

    A model on your own computer needs room for Spellmind's instructions: in Ollama, set the context to 16K or more (OLLAMA_CONTEXT_LENGTH=16384). Without web search, its facts come from memory.
